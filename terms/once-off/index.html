Residential Terms - Once-Off Work

These terms apply to work carried out by The Trustee for Green Fern Gardening Services Trust (ABN 29 279 087 766), trading as Green Fern Gardening Services (“Green Fern”, “we”), for the person named in the quote (“the Client”, “you”), at the property named in the quote.

1. Quote and scope

The quote sets out the work and price. Quotes are valid for the period shown on the quote. Only the work listed is included.

Any extra work is agreed with you, in writing (email or message is fine), with a price, before we do it.

2. Access and your property

Please make sure we can reach the work area on the agreed day, and that any gates, pets and vehicles are out of the way. If we can’t get in, or the site isn’t ready, we may reschedule and charge a reasonable call-out fee.

Tell us before work starts about anything we should know, such as irrigation, underground services (gas, water, power, drainage, septic), soft ground, hazards, or pets. We take reasonable care, but we are not responsible for damage to services or items that weren’t marked or disclosed to us.

3. Scheduling and weather

Dates and times are estimates. We may reschedule because of weather, equipment issues, illness or safety, and will give you as much notice as we reasonably can.

4. Materials and green waste

Materials remain our property until paid for in full. Where we supply plants, mulch, softfall or similar, we may substitute an equivalent product if the specified one is unavailable. Green waste removal is included only where the quote says so.

5. Tree work

For tree removal or major pruning, you confirm that you own the tree or have the owner’s permission. You are responsible for any council permit or overlay approvals unless the quote says we will arrange them, and for discussing the work with neighbours where relevant.

6. Payment

We invoice on completion of the work. No deposit is required unless the quote says otherwise. Invoices are payable within 7 days. If an invoice is overdue we may stop further work, and we may recover reasonable costs of collection.

7. Plants and lawns

Plants and lawn are living things, and we can’t guarantee they will survive, particularly without watering or in extreme weather.

8. Changes and cancellation

You can cancel or reschedule before work starts with at least 48 hours’ notice at no charge. If we have already ordered special-order materials for you, you will need to pay for those.

9. Liability and your rights

We take reasonable care and carry public liability insurance with Youi, to a limit of $10,000,000. We are not responsible for pre-existing conditions, natural plant or lawn failure, or damage caused by weather or events outside our control.

Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law, including the consumer guarantees that our services are provided with due care and skill.

10. Privacy and photos

We use your contact details only to quote, schedule and invoice for the work. We may take photos of completed work for our records and marketing, without identifying you or your address. Tell us if you’d rather we didn’t.

11. If something isn’t right

Please tell us as soon as you can, ideally within 7 days, and we will work with you to put it right. These terms are governed by the laws of Victoria.

12. Acceptance

You accept these terms when you accept our quote (including by email or message), pay an invoice, or let us start work, whichever happens first.
